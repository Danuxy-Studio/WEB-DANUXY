## 1. Arah Utama

`danuxy.com` diposisikan sebagai website resmi **Danuxy Studio**.

Bukan sekadar website jasa dan bukan website yang dipenuhi iklan.

Prinsip:

## Official Brand Identity

**Brand:** Danuxy Studio

**Official tagline:** `BUILD • CREATE • GROW`

### Visual identity

Danuxy uses the new `D` + forward/speed mark as its official logo direction.

**Official palette:**
- Sky Blue (Primary): `#4ED1FF`
- Blue (Secondary): `#2EA8FF`
- Light Blue (Accent): `#E6F4FF`
- Dark Navy: primary dark background/supporting color

### Logo rules
- Use the clean/minimal logo variant as the main website logo.
- Use the icon-only variant for favicon, compact navigation, and small UI.
- Do not stretch, distort, rotate, or unnecessarily recolor the logo.
- Avoid excessive glow.
- Keep the logo readable at small sizes.

### Brand copy
`BUILD • CREATE • GROW` is the official brand tagline.

The supporting website statement may be:

> Building things, breaking things, and vibe coding my way through it.

The tagline represents the brand; the supporting statement represents the personality of the website.

### Visual direction
- Dark navy foundation
- Sky blue and blue accents
- Light blue highlights
- Clean, solid, professional shapes
- Subtle gradients only where useful
- Minimal glow
- Strong contrast
- Responsive and lightweight

Avoid purple as a primary brand color, excessive neon/glow, and generic SaaS-style visual clutter.

# 10. Prinsip Konten

Semua konten harus mengikuti aturan:

1. Gunakan project Danuxy Studio yang benar-benar ada.
2. Jangan mengarang statistik.
3. Jangan mengarang jumlah user.
4. Jangan mengarang testimonial.
5. Jangan mengklaim pencapaian yang belum terjadi.
6. Jangan memakai lorem ipsum.
7. Jangan membuat artikel AI generik hanya untuk mengejar SEO.
8. Utamakan pengalaman, tutorial, dan dokumentasi nyata.
9. Gunakan bahasa Indonesia yang natural.
10. English hanya digunakan jika memang cocok untuk branding atau istilah teknis.
